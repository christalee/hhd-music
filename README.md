# hhd-music
Transcriptions of music for High Holy Days services, 2019

I volunteered to sing with a local synagogue for their Rosh Hashanah, Kol Nidrei, and Yom Kippur morning services. Some of the music we were rehearsing was handwritten or used altered lyrics or melodies. I was inspired to transcribe the pages that were hardest to read, using [LilyPond](http://lilypond.org/website/index.html), a LaTeX-inspired program for typesetting music.

Although I've proofed the transliterations against other publications, I don't read Hebrew and so errors may have been introduced and inconsistencies remain. Similarly, I haven't researched the provenance of this music in any depth. In some files I've skipped the cantor's melody, as these pages are for the choir. I welcome all comments and suggestions for improvement.
